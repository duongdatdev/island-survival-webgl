## Delivery and product gates still open

1. **Human campaign/content balance:** target 8–12 active hours and meaningful unlock/discovery every 45–90 minutes are not measured. Supplied-resource integration execution times do not estimate a first-time player's experience. Do not add repeated gathering, arbitrary wait timers or real-day locks to manufacture duration.
2. **Five first-time playtests:** P01 accepted participation; P01–P05 have no session records or completed durations. Follow `playtest-protocol.vi.md`; retain unfinished sessions and help requests. A single volunteer does not satisfy the five-player median gate.
3. **Complete physical expedition:** continuous land-to-shore-to-wreck transit now reaches all eight discoveries and reads the submerged manifest on one seed. Individual cave/station geometry and interactions have separate checks; continuous exploration of all interiors and native multi-seed traversal remain unproven.
4. **Polish feedback:** human navigation clarity, recurring survival/production decisions, audio mix, readability and comfort remain to be assessed on the fixed build. Keep P01's build/origin stable while development continues separately; record exact versions for any later candidate.

M0–M5 have substantial implementation and subsystem/integration evidence. M6 and overall completion remain open. No change to the original objective or acceptance thresholds is authorized by this audit.
